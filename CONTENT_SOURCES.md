The ages retain the published ranges; staff should clarify boundaries and current individual guidance. The article's outdated District 1 address/hours are not reused. The short bilingual guide appears on the homepage and First Visit page.

Site data now uses a server with D1 and R2. Blog drafts are separate from their published snapshots; existing three editorial stories are seeded once outside schema migrations. HTML is escaped; uploads accept JPG/PNG/WebP signatures and are limited to 6 MiB. Draft images and editing/analytics endpoints require the approved owner identity.

Analytics requires visitor consent and respects Do Not Track. It records first-party browser identifiers, sessions, page views, CTA impressions (50% visibility), and CTA clicks. When signed in, the platform-verified display name may accompany the visit. No IP, form input, email address, full referrer, or URL query strings are stored in analytics. A labelled share link indicates the source link, not verified recipient identity. External link clicks do not prove the external destination was viewed or any purchase/booking occurred.

Statistics are genuine recorded events; synthetic workflow tests run against a separate in-memory SQLite fixture. Tracking captures consenting browser activity and is not a census of all visitors. A browser can represent more than one person, and one person can use multiple browsers. The owner-only demo audience and the existing GitHub publication are preserved.
